Local by design

Your words stay
on your Mac.

Writing suggestions run on your device. Choose a Qwen 3.5 model in Settings, or use the built-in predictor, spelling fixes and shortcuts without downloading one.

Adjust the tone, add your own abbreviations and choose which apps FreeTypist works in. Its Playground lets you try suggestions before using them elsewhere.

Model downloads and update checks need an internet connection. Optional clipboard and window context are processed locally. You control these in Settings.

Up and typing.

  1. Install FreeTypist.Open the download and drag FreeTypist into Applications.
  2. Follow the setup wizard.Download and test the recommended local model, then allow Accessibility access for your installed copy.
  3. Try it in Playground.Press Tab for the full suggestion, Option–Tab for the next word, or Escape to dismiss it. New installations enable emoji shortcuts by default.

Future releases arrive through the app’s signed updater. Find “Check for Updates…” in the menu bar, or enable automatic checks in General.
